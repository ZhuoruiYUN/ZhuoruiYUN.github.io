Team-captain project for the 2026 National Undergraduate Electronics Design Contest, awarded a Provincial Third Prize.

- Built a K230/CanMV vision-feedback pipeline that detects the ball and returns position data for closed-loop balancing control.
- Developed MSPM0G3507 firmware integrating encoders, motor drivers, OLED, UART/I2C communication, incremental PID, lap-stop detection, and fault handling.
- Led on-site system integration, hardware debugging, and final competition execution.
