## [Quadhopper: Dynamics-Informed RL for a Monopedal Hopping Quadcopter](/projects/quadhopper/)
**Undergraduate Researcher, Bio-inspired Mechatronics Laboratory** · Jul 2025 - Present

- Trained a direct estimated-state-to-motor PPO policy in Isaac Lab with 4,096 parallel environments and 100 Hz four-motor control.
- Built a 183 g STM32F103C8T6 platform and deployed an ONNX policy with MPU6050 sensing, nRF24L01 communication, and eight-camera Vicon motion capture.
- Validated repeatable commanded-height hopping and reduced cycle-averaged measured electrical power by 30.7% relative to tuned PID in representative tests.
