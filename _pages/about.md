I am interested in building intelligent robotic systems that move reliably from simulation to real hardware. My work spans reinforcement learning, embedded control, robotic hardware, motion control, and experimental validation.

My current research focuses on **sim-to-real reinforcement learning**, agile robotic locomotion, and hardware validation. In the Quadhopper project, I work on dynamics-informed policies for a monopedal hopping quadcopter, from Isaac Lab training through ONNX deployment and Vicon-based physical experiments.

I also build embedded robotic systems through the RoboMaster KongFu Team, a 4-DOF robotic arm teaching pendant, and a K230/CanMV vision-based ball-balancing cart for the National Undergraduate Electronics Design Contest.
